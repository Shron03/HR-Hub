### Slide 2: Industry Problem & Opportunity
- **The Challenge**:
  - Legacy HR evaluations rely on subjective quarterly reviews and fragmented spreadsheets.
  - Lack of real-time visibility into attendance trends, task throughput, and department efficiency.
  - At-risk employees are identified too late; high performers miss timely recognition.
- **The Solution (HR Hub)**:
  - Centralized, glassmorphic analytics cockpit.
  - Real-time automated calculation of composite KPI scores.
  - Smart diagnostic recommendations engine for proactive decision-making.

---

### Slide 3: Architecture & Technology Stack
- **Frontend Architecture**:
  - React 18+ (Vite) with component modularity.
  - Cyber-Executive Glassmorphism with Tailwind CSS & ambient lighting mesh.
  - Chart.js 4+ with glowing neon canvas gradients.
- **Backend Architecture**:
  - Node.js & Express.js RESTful API.
  - Mongoose ODM with dual-mode MongoDB engine (MongoDB Atlas + In-Memory fallback).
  - Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with 1-click Demo Switcher.
- **Reporting Engine**:
  - Automated client-side vector PDF generation via jsPDF and CSV export.

### Slide 5: Module 2 — Performance Tracking System
- **Weighted KPI Scoring Logic**:
  - Attendance Rate: **20%**
  - Task Completion Rate: **25%**
  - Productivity Score: **25%**
  - Project Contribution: **15%**
  - Work Efficiency: **15%**
- **Evaluation Tiers**:
  - Exceptional ($\ge 92\%$), Strong Performer ($82-91\%$), Meets Expectations ($70-81\%$), Needs Improvement ($60-69\%$), Critical Attention ($< 60\%$).
- **Interactive Features**:
  - Real-time KPI Simulator & Slider Tuner.
  - Live task checklist with instant completion toggles and dynamic task rate recalculation.
